## The Live Event Calendar

The event layer of Aion 2 runs on NCSoft's content cadence: seasonal events anchored to the real-world calendar, celebration events marking the game's milestones, and the recurring limited-time formats the game rotates through its live-service cycle. Each event on the schedule carries the three facts that matter — its **window** (start and end dates; limited-time means the window is the entire point), its **reward structure** (what participation and completion pay, and what the store exchanges look like), and its **time commitment** (the daily/weekly shape of participating without wrecking the rest of your play).

The planning logic the community applies, condensed: **evaluate every event against your progression's current bottleneck** — an event whose rewards overlap your grinding path is a multiplier on hours you were spending anyway; one whose rewards do not fit is a polite decline, and declining is free. The failure pattern is the opposite discipline: treating every live event as mandatory because it is temporary, and exhausting yourself across a calendar you never chose. Events are offers, not obligations — the schedule's job is to let you choose with your eyes open.

## The Reset Tables

## Reading the Calendar Strategically

Three horizon checks that make the calendar a tool rather than a wallpaper. **The ending-soon scan** (weekly, two minutes): what expires in the next two weeks that your progression wants — claimed or consciously skipped, never discovered expired. **The overlap scan** (when events stack): two live events competing for the same play hours is a choice, and the reward-per-hour comparison — run against your bottleneck — usually makes it obvious. **The gap scan**: the quiet periods between marquee events are the natural windows for the long-horizon grinds (the build planner's acquisition plans live in gaps), and scheduling your heavy farming into the calendar's valleys is how the whole year compounds instead of lurching.

## FAQ

**How often does Aion 2 run events?** Continuously at the content-cadence level — typically one or more live events at any time, rotating on the seasonal and milestone calendar. The live table is the answer; the rhythm generalizes poorly.

**When is the daily reset?** Server-anchored and constant — the reset table carries the time; confirm your own server's offset once and anchor your day to it permanently.

**Should I do every event?** No — the discipline is bottleneck-matching: events that multiply hours you were spending anyway are taken, the rest are declined. The calendar exists to make that choice informed, not to fill it.

**What happens to event currency when an event ends?** Per-event rules, published with the event — the reward-structure notes flag the convert-or-expire mechanics in advance. Check before hoarding.

**How do I catch schedule changes fast?** Dated tables and official announcements — this page tracks the verified cycle, and the as-of date is the freshness contract. After patches, verify before committing.
